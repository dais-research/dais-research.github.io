abstract: |-
  A personalized recommendation system that expands candidate literature by
  following citation links from user-provided seed papers and identifies
  scholarly publications, patents, and technical reports relevant to each
  researcher's interests. It combines citation metadata, journal-quality
  indicators, and large-language-model-based relevance assessment while
  supporting abstract retrieval, Korean translation, and iterative
  recommendation based on user feedback. Registered to the Korea Institute of
  Energy Research as a work made for hire (creation 2026-07-09, registration
  2026-08-18).
